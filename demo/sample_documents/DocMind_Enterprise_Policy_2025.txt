# DocMind Global Enterprise Employment & Operations Policy (2025 Edition)
Document Code: DOC-POL-2025-V1
Category: HR
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Section 1: Workplace Model & Remote Work
Employees assigned to corporate headquarters or regional hubs are required to maintain a hybrid schedule with a minimum of 3 designated days per week on-site in the office. Remote work is limited to a maximum of 2 days per week, subject to direct manager approval. Core working hours are designated between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM in the employee's local time zone.

Section 2: Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Accrual
Full-time personnel accrue paid vacation leave at a base rate of 1.25 days per completed calendar month of active service, providing an annual entitlement of 15 business days. A maximum rollover of 5 unused vacation days is permitted into the subsequent calendar year; any additional unused days expire without financial compensation. Sick leave accrues separately at 5 paid business days per calendar year.

Section 3: Home Office Equipment & Technology Allowance
Upon onboarding, employees eligible for hybrid scheduling receive a one-time technology stipend of $500 to assist with peripheral ergonomic accessories, external monitors, or desktop peripherals. All standard computing hardware, including company-issued laptops and security tokens, remains the exclusive property of DocMind Global.

Section 4: Hardware Maintenance & Refresh Cycle
Standard corporate laptop hardware follows a strict 4-year lifecycle refresh schedule. Equipment replacements requested prior to 48 months require department director sign-off and documented hardware malfunction.
